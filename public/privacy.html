Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Meal Deck is a free meal planning app. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: your name, email address and profile picture, shared with us when you sign in with Google. We only use your email to identify your account.
  • What you add to the app: your meals, ingredients, photos, weekly plan, grocery lists and settings.

How it is used

Your information is used only to run the app: signing you in and saving your meals, plans and lists so they are there when you open Meal Deck on another device. We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and the app is hosted by Vercel. Each account can only read and change its own data. Google handles the sign-in itself, and we never see your Google password.

Deleting your data

You can erase all of your meals, plans and lists at any time using "Reset all data" in the app's Settings. To have your account and everything saved with it removed, email us at the address below and we will delete it.

Contact

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
